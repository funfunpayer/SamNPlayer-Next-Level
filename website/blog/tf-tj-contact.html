Tf/Tj + contact vibration

Two ROIs measure distance; contact can drive vibe on Neo 2 without inventing motion.

Sam Neo 2 is not a single-axis toy. Native .samn carries general motion plus vibration and suction curves, recipes, and strength presets.

Tf/Tj distance

Mark two regions (or accept a two-ROI suggestion). The generator tracks distance over time for suction-style scripts that follow the scene instead of a single blob.

Contact vibration (opt-in)

When enabled, vibe intensity follows contact in the measured span — stored on the recipe so Play can re-derive axes. It is a deliberate recipe, not a silent post-process mystery.

Subjective “feel” on real hardware is still an owner test item; use Device diagnostics and Mock first, then a short Neo 2 session.
