AI proposes, tracking writes

Optional local ONNX can suggest regions and profiles. It never owns the output file alone.

Many tools ship “AI Funscripts” that look fine until you watch them against the film. SamNPlayer draws a hard line: classical tracking writes .samn / .funscript. AI is an optional helper on your machine.

What AI may do

  • Propose an ROI (or two for Tf/Tj)
  • Suggest a motion profile
  • Offer a second opinion next to Quality Doctor

What AI must not do

  • No second, AI-only path that silently authors the script
  • No model bundled in the binary, no surprise cloud download
  • No auto-commit of an unvalidated region guess

Flow stays: video → optional ONNX suggestion → CSRT / flow / grid_lk → Quality Doctor. You stay in the loop on accept / reject.
