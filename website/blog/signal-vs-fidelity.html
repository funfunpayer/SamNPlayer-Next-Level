Signal quality ≠ motion fidelity

A smooth script is not automatically a true script. Two questions, two tools.

Signal quality

Is the funscript a plausible control signal? Jitter, impossible jumps, gaps, speed limits, tracking-loss fraction, device density. That is what Quality Doctor / Script Doctor report.

A high score does not prove the curve matches the video.

Motion fidelity

Does the signal faithfully represent motion in the film (or a FunGen / human reference)? Rhythm, phase lag, turning points, amplitude, correlation. That is golden-clip / phase-compare territory — a different number.

Product rule

We never market a Quality Doctor score as “matches the video.” Read the rail as: safe to send to the device? Then judge fidelity with your eyes (and benchmarks when you have references).
