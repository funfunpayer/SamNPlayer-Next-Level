FAQ

Short answers. Product UI and this site stay English.

What platforms are supported?

Windows and Linux amd64 GUI + CLI now (prefer portable archives). macOS and a mobile player (play + device only, no Generate) are planned — not shipped.

What does it cost?

Nothing. SamNPlayer is freeware for private, non-commercial use — full Generate and Neo 2 .samn play, no licence key, no trial limits. If it helps you, a voluntary donation (Patreon / PayPal) keeps development going. See Free licence & donations.

Does anything leave my PC?

No by design — local-only, no telemetry, no bundled model, no cloud AI calls. Optional ONNX stays on your machine.

Do I need ffmpeg installed?

Not if you use the portable release (ffmpeg next to the GUI) or Settings → Install video tools. Details: Portable zip on a clean PC.

.samn or .funscript?

.samn is the native Neo 2 document (multi-axis + recipe). .funscript remains community import/export. You can bake and export either way from Play.

Is this open source?

No. SamNPlayer is free but closed source: the binaries are free under the EULA, the application source is private. Older MIT copies of past public trees may still exist elsewhere.

Where do I get help?

Questions and issues on the showcase GitHub. Start with Getting started and Neo 2 + Bluetooth.
