Sam Neo 2 + Bluetooth

First-class device support: BLE, Intiface/Buttplug, and Mock for dry runs.

Hardware tip (Windows 11)

Intiface recommends an external USB dongle with an antenna, not bare onboard radios. Common picks: TP-Link UB500 or Asus USB-BT500. See also Intiface Bluetooth hardware guidance.

Connect in SamNPlayer

  1. Power the Neo 2 / Neo 2 Pro and put it in pairing / connect mode per SVAKOM docs.
  2. In the app, use the top-bar device control or the Device tab.
  3. Choose BLE direct, Intiface if you already run Central, or Mock to test UI without hardware.
  4. Run a short play session and watch vibration / suction meters — they should move with the script.

Why Neo 2 is special here

Native .samn scripts carry general + vibration + suction axes, contact-vibration recipes, and strength presets. Community .funscript remains import/export. Tf/Tj generation can drive contact-triggered vibe — see Tf/Tj + contact vibration.

If connect fails

  • Move closer; USB 3 ports and metal desks often hurt BLE.
  • Close other apps that hold the radio (vendor apps, other Buttplug clients).
  • Try Intiface Central as a bridge, then connect SamNPlayer to Intiface.
  • Use Mock to confirm playback and meters before blaming the radio.
